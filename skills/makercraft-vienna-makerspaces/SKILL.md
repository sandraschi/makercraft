# Vienna Makerspaces Reference & How-To

## 1. Metalab (1010 Wien, Rathausstrasse 6)
- **Profile:** Community-run hackerspace (founded 2006), open culture, electronics lab, 3D printing, CNC/lasers, software/hardware hacking, weekly meetups.
- **How to start:** Drop by during open evenings or public events, introduce yourself, ask for a tour. Membership is monthly (supports the space), with 24/7 keycard access for members.
- **Best for:** Embedded electronics, soldering/SMD rework, Bus Pirate / logic analyzer debugging, Linux/Klipper tinkering, late-night hacking.

## 2. Happylab (1020 Wien, Schoengasse 15-17)
- **Profile:** Large structured makerspace / Fab Lab (~900+ m2) with industrial laser cutters, 3D printers (FDM/SLA), CNC routers, wood/metal workshops, vinyl/textile tools.
- **How to start:** Book an **intro/safety briefing (Einschulung)** online for the specific machine class you want (Laser, CNC, Wood, 3D Print). Machine time is booked via their member portal.
- **Best for:** Laser cutting (acrylic/plywood), large-format CNC milling, clean repeatable production, beginners wanting formal machine training.

## 3. Maker Austria (1050 Wien, Schoenbrunner Strasse 125)
- **Profile:** Non-profit open workshop (~800 m2) combining wood, metal, ceramics, textiles, 3D printing, laser cutting, and electronics.
- **How to start:** Register for a safety orientation / workshop tour and machine-specific briefings; day passes and memberships available.
- **Best for:** Mixed-material builds (wood + metal + electronics enclosures), pottery/ceramics, and collaborative craft projects.

## Makerspace Prep Checklist (Before You Go)
1. **Verify files at home:** Run `gcode_lint` / `gcode_check` on G-code, or export clean DXF/SVG (hairline strokes, converted to paths, mm units) for laser cutters.
2. **Bring your own consumables:** Filament/resin (if allowed), clean acrylic/birch plywood (no PVC on laser cutters - toxic chlorine gas!), endmills, and USB stick.
3. **Never bypass safety briefings or exhaust interlocks.** Clean the bed/spoilboard and empty dust/scrap bins when finished.
